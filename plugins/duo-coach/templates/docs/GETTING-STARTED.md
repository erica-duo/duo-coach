# Getting the most out of your AI co-founder

*One page. Read once, keep forever.*

## Opening it

Open the **Claude Code app** → open your `{firstname}-brain` folder (it's in your recent folders). Done. No terminal, no setup — Claude reads your whole brain every time.

## The one habit that matters

**Talk to it like a sharp new hire, not a search engine.** Give it context and a goal, not keywords.

- 😕 "email client"
- ✅ "Draft a follow-up email to Sarah from yesterday's call — she was worried about timeline, reassure her and confirm the Thursday deliverable."

## Five things to try this week

1. **"What do you know about my business?"** — see what's loaded in your brain
2. **"Prep me for my next call with [name]"** — paste the agenda or context
3. **"Draft a LinkedIn post about [something you said this week]"** — it knows your voice once Erica pushes your voice doc
4. **Type `/`** — see your slash commands. Each is a ready-made workflow.
5. **"Build me a skill that does [weekly task you hate]"** — yes, really

## The three commands to remember

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/help` | Refresher on all of this, anytime |
| `/handoff` | Saves your spot before you close; run it again next session to resume |
| Shift+Tab | Plan mode — Claude shows its plan before doing anything big |

## What it can and can't see

**Can see:** everything in this folder — your context docs, skills, notes, past session memory.
**Can't see:** your email, calendar, Slack, or Notion (unless Duo wired them up). If you want something connected, ask Erica.

## When to ping Duo

Automation stopped working, want something new built, billing questions, or anything feels off — Slack Erica or Nick. Don't wrestle with it.

## One warning

Never paste API keys or passwords into the chat. If Claude ever needs a credential, the answer is always "send it to Erica in Slack."
